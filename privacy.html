Privacy notice

This notice describes Dmitry Kravchenko's personal Hermes integration with his own Gmail and Google Calendar account. It is not a notice for all Hermes software installations or a statement on behalf of Google or Nous Research.

Data accessed

When authorized and requested by the account owner, the integration may access email message content, sender and recipient details, message metadata and labels, and calendar event details. Google access tokens and refresh tokens are used to maintain authorization. Authorized permissions may support email or calendar changes, but the initial operating mode is review-only and changes require the owner's explicit instructions.

Use of data

Data is used for the owner's requested assistance, including summaries, inbox review, follow-up identification, and calendar review. It is not collected to offer a service to other Google users.

Processing and storage

Credentials are stored by the owner's Hermes installation, not on this public site. Relevant email and calendar content may be sent to the AI model provider configured by the owner to generate responses. Depending on the configuration, content, summaries, or derived information may also be retained in chat history, logs, or memory services. Those services have their own applicable processing and retention terms. This notice does not claim that all processing occurs locally.

Public hosting

GitHub Pages hosts only these explanatory documents. Mailbox content, calendar entries, OAuth client secrets, and Google access or refresh tokens are not intended for publication in this site or its repository. Visiting this site does not authorize Google account access. GitHub may process ordinary hosting request data under its own privacy statement.

Control and revocation

The owner can stop the integration and revoke its authorization in Google Account settings. Revocation prevents further authorized access but does not automatically erase existing chat history, logs, memory entries, or copies already processed by configured services. Those must be reviewed and removed separately through the relevant local or provider controls.

Operator

The operator is Dmitry-Kravchenko on GitHub. This integration is for the operator's personal account only.
