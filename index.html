Hermes — Personal Google Integration

This is a personal integration operated by Dmitry Kravchenko to connect his own Hermes assistant to his own Gmail and Google Calendar account. It is not a public service, and it is not an official website of Google or Nous Research.

Purpose

The integration is intended to review email, summarize messages, identify follow-up tasks, and read calendar events. Changes to email or calendar data require the account owner's explicit instructions. The initial operating mode is review-only, although the Google authorization may grant broader permissions for future approved actions.

Authorization and processing

The account owner authorizes access directly with Google. The integration runs through the owner's Hermes installation. Depending on that installation's configuration, selected email or calendar content can be processed by AI model providers and memory services. This site does not connect to the Google account and does not contain account credentials or mailbox data.
